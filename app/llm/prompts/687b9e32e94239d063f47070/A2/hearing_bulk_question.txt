❓ **QUESTION FORMAT**

**Use ONLY these question types:**

1. **"fill_in_blank"** - User must type a specific detail they heard (NO options). Best for concrete facts.
2. **"short_answer"** - User writes a brief answer (1-5 words) based on understanding. Best for comprehension questions.
3. **"mcq"** - Multiple choice with exactly four options labelled `a)`…`d)`.
4. **"true_false"** - Two options: "Richtig", "Falsch".

**For fill_in_blank questions:**
- Ask for **specific factual information** from the audio:
  - 📞 Phone numbers (e.g., "0176 234 5678")
  - ⏰ Times including ranges (e.g., "von 9 bis 17 Uhr")
  - 📅 Dates with months (e.g., "15. Oktober", "Montag, den 12. Mai")
  - 🔢 Prices, ages, quantities (e.g., "25 Euro", "drei Tage")
  - 📍 Full addresses (e.g., "Hauptstraße 12, Berlin")
  - 👤 Full names (e.g., "Frau Schmidt", "Herr Müller")
  - 🎫 Reference numbers, booking codes
- Question must be very clear and specific (e.g., "Wie lautet die Adresse?", "Wann beginnt der Kurs?")

**For short_answer questions:**
- Ask about comprehension, reasons, sequences (e.g., "Warum kann die Frau nicht kommen?", "Was empfiehlt der Mann?")
- Expected answers are 1-5 words (e.g., "Sie ist krank", "den roten Pullover", "am Wochenende")
- Test understanding of reasons, opinions, recommendations, not just hearing facts

**Important for BOTH types:**
- Set `"options": null` or `"options": []`
- The `correct_answer` must be the exact text as spoken in the audio (for fill_in_blank) or a clear expected answer (for short_answer)
- Mix both types in each batch (35% fill_in_blank, 35% short_answer, 30% rest)
- Keep the question text concise (max 25 German words) and A2-appropriate

🎯 **CRITICAL: AVOID DIRECT KEYWORD MATCHING**

**Questions must test COMPREHENSION, not just keyword recognition!**

❌ **BAD (Too obvious):**
- Audio: "Der Kurs kostet 150 Euro"
- Question: "Wie viel kostet der Kurs?"
- Answer: "150 Euro"
- ❌ User can answer by just hearing "150 Euro"

✅ **GOOD (Requires understanding):**
- Audio: "Der Kurs beginnt nach den Sommerferien, am ersten September."
- Question: "Wann beginnt der Kurs?"
- Answer: "1. September" (requires connecting "nach den Sommerferien" to specific date)

- Audio: "Wir haben von 9 bis 17 Uhr geöffnet, aber Mittwochs schließen wir um 14 Uhr."
- Question: "Bis wann kann man am Mittwoch kommen?"
- Answer: "14 Uhr" (requires understanding exception to general rule)

- Audio: "Die Fahrt dauert normalerweise zwei Stunden, aber mit Umsteigen in Frankfurt brauchen Sie drei Stunden."
- Question: "Wie lange dauert die Fahrt mit Umsteigen?"
- Answer: "3 Stunden" (requires distinguishing between two different scenarios)

- Audio: "Für Studenten gibt es einen Rabatt von 20%. Der normale Preis ist 50 Euro."
- Question: "Wie viel zahlen Studenten?"
- Answer: "40 Euro" (requires calculation, not just hearing a number)

**A2 Indirect Answer Guidelines:**
- Time/date inferences: "nächste Woche Dienstag" → specific date, "nach dem Wochenende" → Monday
- Conditional information: "außer sonntags" → list weekdays, "nur für Mitglieder" → eligibility
- Comparisons: "länger als", "früher als" → requires choosing between options
- Calculations: discounts, durations, quantities that need mental math
- Exceptions: "normalerweise... aber..." → answer should reflect the exception
- Make connections moderately challenging but keep A2-level vocabulary
