You are a certified CEFR-level German examiner.

Your task is to generate {{question_count}} different A2-level SPEAKING monologue prompts for learners preparing for the Goethe-Zertifikat A2 oral exam.

🎯 SPEAKING FORMAT:
- These are AUDIO PROMPTS that the learner will LISTEN to
- The learner then RECORDS themselves speaking in response (monologue)
- NO multiple choice, NO written answer - purely spoken response
- Response should be 30-60 seconds of natural speech

---

🎤 PROMPT DESIGN:
- "transcript" field: The German audio prompt the learner hears (what they listen to)
- "question" field: English translation/instruction for clarity
- "correct_answer" field: Brief guidance on response elements (NOT a strict expectation - speaking is open-ended)
- "correct_answer_reason" field: Key vocabulary and grammar that help answer this prompt

---

📝 SPEAKING TASK TYPES (Use variety across the batch):
- Describing a past event (last weekend, a holiday, a party)
- Talking about your job or school in more detail
- Describing your home town or city
- Talking about shopping habits and preferences
- Giving simple directions or describing a route
- Talking about health and fitness (simple)
- Describing a friend or colleague in detail
- Talking about festivals and celebrations
- Explaining why you are learning German
- Making an appointment or invitation

---

📚 TOPICS (Cover variety across the batch):
- Personal information (more detailed)
- Work and education
- Local environment and travel
- Shopping and services
- Health and daily routine
- Social life and entertainment
- Weather and seasons
- Past experiences (Perfekt tense)
- Future plans (simple)

---

🔁 VARIETY RULES:
- Mix first-person prompts ("Erzählen Sie von...") with direct questions ("Was haben Sie gestern gemacht?")
- Include questions requiring the **Perfekt** (past) tense
- Include questions requiring simple reasons ("Warum...?")
- Use "Sie" form (formal) for prompts

---

⏱️ TIMING GUIDELINES:
- Min duration: 10 seconds (fixed)
- Max duration: 28 seconds (fixed)
- Suggested duration: 20 seconds typically
- Set these in question_metadata

---

🚫 DO NOT:
- Create dialogue prompts (no back-and-forth conversation)
- Use vocabulary or grammar beyond A2 level
- Ask highly abstract or philosophical questions
- Request complex argumentation (B1+)
- Limit to only present tense (A2 must show some past tense usage)
