❓ **QUESTION FORMAT**

**Use ONLY these question types:**

1. **"fill_in_blank"** - User must type a specific detail they heard (NO options). Best for concrete facts.
2. **"short_answer"** - User writes a brief answer (1-5 words) based on understanding. Best for comprehension questions.
3. **"mcq"** - Multiple choice with exactly four options labelled `a)`…`d)`.
4. **"true_false"** - Two options: "Richtig", "Falsch".

**For fill_in_blank questions:**
- Ask for **specific factual information** from the audio:
  - 📞 Phone numbers (e.g., "0176 234 5678")
  - ⏰ Times (e.g., "14:30" or "vierzehn Uhr dreißig")
  - 📅 Dates (e.g., "15. Oktober" or "Montag")
  - 🔢 Numbers (prices, ages, quantities)
  - 📍 Addresses (e.g., "Hauptstraße 12")
  - 👤 Names (e.g., "Müller", "Anna")
- Question must be very clear and specific (e.g., "Wie lautet die Telefonnummer?", "Um wie viel Uhr...?")

**For short_answer questions:**
- Ask about comprehension, not just facts (e.g., "Wohin fährt die Frau?", "Was möchte der Mann kaufen?")
- Expected answers are 1-5 words (e.g., "nach Berlin", "ein Ticket", "zum Arzt")
- Test understanding of the situation, not just hearing a specific word

**Important for BOTH types:**
- Set `"options": null` or `"options": []` 
- The `correct_answer` must be the exact text as spoken in the audio (for fill_in_blank) or a clear expected answer (for short_answer)
- Mix both types in each batch (35% fill_in_blank, 35% short_answer, 30% rest)
- Keep the question text short (max 20 German words) and A1-friendly

🎯 **CRITICAL: AVOID DIRECT KEYWORD MATCHING**

**Questions must test COMPREHENSION, not just keyword recognition!**

❌ **BAD (Too obvious):**
- Audio: "Erwachsene zahlen 8 Euro"
- Question: "Wie viel zahlen Erwachsene?"
- Answer: "8 Euro"
- ❌ User can answer without understanding

✅ **GOOD (Requires understanding):**
- Audio: "Das Museum öffnet morgen um zehn Uhr."
- Question: "Wann öffnet das Museum?"
- Answer: "10:00 Uhr" (requires understanding "morgen um zehn")

- Audio: "Die Praxis ist heute geschlossen. Kommen Sie bitte am Montag."
- Question: "Wann ist die Praxis offen?"
- Answer: "Montag" (requires understanding "heute geschlossen" vs "am Montag")

- Audio: "Im Sommer haben wir von 9 bis 18 Uhr geöffnet."
- Question: "Bis wann ist im Juli geöffnet?"
- Answer: "18 Uhr" (requires knowing Juli = Sommer)

**A1 Indirect Answer Guidelines:**
- Time expressions: "morgen" → specific time, "heute Abend" → "19:00"
- Date references: "nächste Woche" → specific day, "am Montag" → date
- Quantity inference: "für zwei Personen" → price calculation
- Location details: audio mentions "Hauptstraße", answer needs house number from different part
- Make the connection simple but require active listening to multiple parts of the audio
