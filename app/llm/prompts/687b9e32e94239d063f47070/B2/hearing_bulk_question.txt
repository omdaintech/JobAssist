- Implicit attitudes: Tone, hedging, discourse markers (zwar...aber, einerseits...andererseits) signal nuanced positions

- Causal inference: Connect implicit cause-effect relationships across multiple sentences
- Abstract synthesis: Combine scattered points to identify underlying argument or assumption
- Attitudinal markers: "kritisch sehen", "Bedenken haben", "befürworten" require inferring from context
