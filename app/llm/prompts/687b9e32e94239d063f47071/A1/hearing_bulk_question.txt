❓ **QUESTION FORMAT**

**Use ONLY these question types:**

1. **"fill_in_blank"** - User must type a specific detail they heard (NO options). Best for concrete facts.
2. **"short_answer"** - User writes a brief answer (1-5 words) based on understanding. Best for comprehension questions.
3. **"mcq"** - Multiple choice with exactly four options labelled `a)`…`d)`.
4. **"true_false"** - Two options: "Vrai", "Faux".

**For fill_in_blank questions:**
- Ask for **specific factual information** from the audio:
  - 📞 Phone numbers (e.g., "06 12 34 56 78")
  - ⏰ Times (e.g., "14h30" or "quatorze heures trente")
  - 📅 Dates (e.g., "15 octobre" or "lundi")
  - 🔢 Numbers (prices, ages, quantities)
  - 📍 Addresses (e.g., "rue de la Paix, 12")
  - 👤 Names (e.g., "Dupont", "Marie")
- Question must be very clear and specific (e.g., "Quel est le numéro de téléphone ?", "À quelle heure...?")

**For short_answer questions:**
- Ask about comprehension, not just facts (e.g., "Où va la femme ?", "Qu'est-ce que l'homme veut acheter ?")
- Expected answers are 1-5 words (e.g., "à Paris", "un billet", "chez le médecin")
- Test understanding of the situation, not just hearing a specific word

**Important for BOTH types:**
- Set `"options": null` or `"options": []` 
- The `correct_answer` must be the exact text as spoken in the audio (for fill_in_blank) or a clear expected answer (for short_answer)
- Mix both types in each batch (35% fill_in_blank, 35% short_answer, 30% rest)
- Keep the question text short (max 20 French words) and A1-friendly

---

🎯 **CRITICAL: AVOID DIRECT KEYWORD MATCHING**

**Questions must test COMPREHENSION, not just keyword recognition!**

❌ **BAD (Too obvious):**
- Audio: "Les adultes paient 8 euros"
- Question: "Combien paient les adultes ?"
- Answer: "8 euros"
- ❌ User can answer without understanding

✅ **GOOD (Requires understanding):**
- Audio: "Le musée ouvre demain à dix heures."
- Question: "Quand ouvre le musée ?"
- Answer: "10h00" (requires understanding "demain à dix")

- Audio: "Le cabinet est fermé aujourd'hui. Venez lundi, s'il vous plaît."
- Question: "Quand le cabinet est-il ouvert ?"
- Answer: "lundi" (requires understanding "fermé aujourd'hui" vs "venez lundi")

**Note**: Avoid requiring multi-step cultural knowledge for A1 (e.g., knowing "juillet = été"). Keep inference to one simple step that's directly stated in the audio.

**A1 Indirect Answer Guidelines:**
- Time expressions: "demain" → specific time, "ce soir" → "19h00"
- Date references: "la semaine prochaine" → specific day, "lundi" → date
- Quantity inference: "pour deux personnes" → price calculation
- Location details: audio mentions "rue de la Paix", answer needs house number from different part
- **IMPORTANT FOR A1**: Keep connections simple and require only ONE-STEP logical thinking (e.g., "demain à dix" = "10h"). Multi-step inferences (e.g., "juillet" → "été" → opening hours) are too complex for A1 and should be avoided. A1 learners need direct or one-step connections only.
